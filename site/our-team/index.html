The people behind your night

Sleep problems rarely have a single cause, so our patients are looked after by specialists from several disciplines.

How we work together

  • Your consultation, sleep study and review are coordinated by one team
  • Results are shared between the specialists involved in your care
  • Referrals to ENT, psychology, psychiatry and other specialists are arranged for you when needed
  • Your GP or referring doctor receives a report, with your consent

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about appointments, sleep studies, insurance and treatment.

Book an appointmentWhatsApp us
Does a sleep study hurt?

No. The sensors are stuck to the skin with gentle tape or paste, with a soft band around the chest and a small sensor on a fingertip. Nothing pierces the skin.

What if I can't sleep with the sensors on?

Almost everyone sleeps enough for a useful result. We need a few hours of sleep, not a perfect night. The room is private and quiet, and you can use the bathroom during the night.

How much does a sleep study cost, and does insurance cover it?

Call or WhatsApp us for the current price. Many medical insurers cover sleep studies with a doctor's referral, and we can help you check your cover and get pre-authorisation before your night.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book?

No. You can book a consultation or sleep assessment with us directly. If you have a referral letter or earlier test results, please bring them.

Where are you located?

We are at 10 Mbaazi Avenue, Lavington, Nairobi. Call us on +254 780 587 851 if you need directions.

What are your opening hours?

The clinic is open monday to saturday, 8:00 am – 5:00 pm. Overnight sleep studies run by appointment: you check in in the evening and go home the next morning.

Do you accept medical insurance?

Many insurers cover consultations and sleep studies. Contact us with your insurer's name and we will help you check your cover and any pre-authorisation needed.

Do you see children?

Yes. Snoring, breathing pauses and restless sleep in children should always be assessed. Call us so we can book the right appointment for your child's age.

Do you sell or rent CPAP machines?

We supply CPAP machines, masks and accessories, and set them up and follow you up after fitting. Ask us about current models, prices and rental options.

Do I need a referral?

You can book a consultation directly. Some insurers ask for a doctor's referral for the sleep study itself, so check with your insurer or ask us.

Can my child have a sleep study?

Children who snore, stop breathing or sleep very restlessly can be assessed. Call us to arrange the right appointment for your child's age; a parent stays with the child overnight.

When will I get my results?

Several hours of recording have to be analysed and reported by a specialist, so results are not ready the same morning. We will book a review appointment to go through the report and your treatment options with you.

Still have a question?

Send us a WhatsApp. A member of our team will reply during clinic hours.

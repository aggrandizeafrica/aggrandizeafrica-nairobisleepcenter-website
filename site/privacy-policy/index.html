Privacy policy

How The Sleep Diagnostic & Treatment Centre collects, uses and protects your personal and health information.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

The Sleep Diagnostic & Treatment Centre ("we", "us"), 10 Mbaazi Avenue, Lavington, Nairobi, Kenya, is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and at our clinic. We process personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number and email address
  • Appointment details, such as the service you need and your preferred date
  • Health information you choose to share when booking, and the clinical information gathered during your care
  • Website usage data, such as pages visited and device type, collected through analytics cookies if you allow them

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange appointments
  • To provide, record and follow up your care
  • To process payments and insurance claims
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations
  • To understand and improve how our website is used

Health information is sensitive personal data. We process it only to provide your care, with your consent, or where the law allows.

Booking through WhatsApp

Our booking form does not store your details on this website. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp with your message, and the message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under their own privacy terms. Please share only what is needed to book. You can always call us instead.

Who we share it with

We share information only when needed for your care or required by law: for example with doctors involved in your treatment, your insurer (for claims you ask us to make), laboratories, or regulators. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Medical records are kept for the period required by Kenyan law and professional guidelines. Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed about how we use your data, to access it, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to some processing, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact us on +254 780 587 851 or at admin@nairobisleepcenter.com. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed for the site to work, plus analytics cookies to understand site usage if they are enabled. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including restricted access to records.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

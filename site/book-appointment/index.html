WhatsApp should have opened with your request. Press send, and we'll reply to confirm your appointment.

What happens next

  1. We reply during clinic hours to confirm a date and time.
  2. For a sleep study, we send preparation instructions and the price, and help check your insurance.
  3. You come in for your consultation or overnight study.
  4. We review your results with you and agree a treatment plan.

About us

Specialist sleep care, made in Nairobi

Since 2018, The Sleep Diagnostic & Treatment Centre has given Kenyans a place to get sleep problems properly measured and treated, close to home.

Book an appointmentWhatsApp us

Why we exist

The Sleep Diagnostic and Treatment Centre (SDTC) is a dedicated sleep laboratory for the diagnosis and treatment of sleep disorders. It was founded in 2018 by three ear, nose and throat surgeons, Prof. Muthure Macharia, Dr Chimmy Omamo-Olende and Dr Samuel M. Nyagah, so that patients in Kenya and the region could have sleep studies close to home.

We are in a quiet, accessible setting on Mbaazi Avenue in Lavington. Our study rooms are designed to feel like your own bedroom, because the more natural your night, the more accurately the results reflect the sleep you get every night.

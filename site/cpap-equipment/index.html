CPAP machines, masks & sleep equipment

CPAP machines

Fixed-pressure machines for sleep apnoea, set to the pressure your sleep study shows you need.

Auto-adjusting CPAP (APAP)

Machines that adjust the pressure breath by breath through the night, often more comfortable to start on.

Request a quote

BiPAP machines

Two-level pressure for people who need more support or find CPAP hard to breathe out against.

Masks and cushions

Nasal, pillow and full-face masks. Fit matters more than anything else, so we fit them in person.

Humidifiers and tubing

Why buy from a sleep centre

Set up properly, supported for the long run

  • Pressure settings based on your own sleep study
  • Masks fitted in person, with different styles to try
  • Follow-up reviews and data downloads to check that therapy is working
  • Help with dryness, leaks, noise and getting used to the mask
  • Spares and replacement parts when you need them

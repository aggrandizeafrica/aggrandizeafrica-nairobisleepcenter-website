About us

Specialist sleep care, made in Nairobi

Since 2019, The Sleep Diagnostic & Treatment Centre has given Kenyans a place to get sleep problems properly measured and treated, close to home.

Book an appointmentWhatsApp us

Why we exist

Sleep disorders are common, serious and often missed. Too many people spend years tired, snoring or lying awake, or are told to "just relax", without anyone measuring what actually happens to them at night.

We set up a dedicated sleep laboratory so that patients in Kenya and the region can have overnight sleep studies locally, and then get treatment from one coordinated team.

Our mission

To help every patient sleep and live better, with accurate diagnosis, evidence-based treatment and personal, respectful care.

Sleep laboratory

Private, comfortable rooms for overnight polysomnography, monitored by trained technologists.

One team

ENT, psychology and sleep technology working from the same results.

Evidence first

Treatment based on your own data and international guidelines.

A night in our sleep lab

Check in at dusk. Leave with data.

An overnight sleep study (polysomnography) is the gold standard for diagnosing sleep apnoea and other sleep disorders. Here is how it works.

  1. Before

    Consultation

    A specialist takes your history, examines your airway and decides which test you need.

  2. Evening

    Check in

    Arrive in comfortable night clothes. Your private room feels more hotel than hospital.

  3. Bedtime

    Painless sensors

    Small sensors on the scalp, face, chest, legs and finger record your sleep. Nothing hurts.

  4. All night

    Watched over

    A sleep technologist monitors breathing, oxygen, heart and brain activity until morning.

  5. After

    Results & plan

    Your study is scored and reported, then reviewed with you alongside a treatment plan.

Treatment

A diagnosis is only half the job. We treat it too.

Your report comes with a plan, and a team to carry it out under one roof and across the Nairobi ENT family.

  • CPAP therapy for sleep apnoea, with machine and mask fitting, and follow-up until it feels natural.
  • ENT and airway treatment for nasal blockage, large tonsils and other structural causes.
  • CBT for insomnia and psychology support, the recommended first treatment for chronic insomnia.
  • Specialist medical management for narcolepsy, restless legs and limb movement disorders.

All FAQs
Does a sleep study hurt?

No. The sensors are stuck to the skin with gentle tape or paste, with a soft band around the chest and a small sensor on a fingertip. Nothing pierces the skin.

What if I can't sleep with the sensors on?

Almost everyone sleeps enough for a useful result. We need a few hours of sleep, not a perfect night. The room is private and quiet, and you can use the bathroom during the night.

How much does a sleep study cost, and does insurance cover it?

Call or WhatsApp us for the current price. Many medical insurers cover sleep studies with a doctor's referral, and we can help you check your cover and get pre-authorisation before your night.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book?

No. You can book a consultation or sleep assessment with us directly. If you have a referral letter or earlier test results, please bring them.
